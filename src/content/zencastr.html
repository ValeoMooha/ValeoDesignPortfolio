Zencastr

Built Zencastr’s design department and all visual processes from scratch, scaling the product from MVP to a $60M valuation. I led a global rebranding, created an Atomic UI design system, and designed the entire product ecosystem: a feature-rich online studio with AI audio/video editing, an ad marketplace, a discovery platform, and an iOS app. During my tenure, the team grew 9x, and the platform became the gold standard for top creators.
